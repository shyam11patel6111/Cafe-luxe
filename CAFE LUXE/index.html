About CAFE LUXE

We are a women-owned business dedicated to creating a welcoming community space where everyone feels at home.

At CAFE LUXE, we believe that luxury should be accessible to all. Our mission is to provide exceptional food and beverages in an inclusive, safe, and welcoming environment. We proudly identify as a women-owned establishment and are committed to being an LGBTQ+ friendly space where diversity is celebrated.

Every dish we serve is crafted with care, using quality ingredients to bring you an affordable luxury experience. Whether you're joining us for a quick coffee, a business meeting, or a leisurely meal with friends, we're here to make your visit memorable.
